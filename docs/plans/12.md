# Night incidents keep the correct priority

## Goal

Customer-facing incidents receive the same priority uplift at night as during business hours, while responder routing remains time-dependent.

## Steps

1. Update `incidentdesk/priority.py` so the customer-facing priority uplift does not depend on business hours.
2. Update `tests/unit/test_priority.py` with focused night-time and weekend assertions, while retaining the existing daytime and timezone coverage.
3. Update `CHANGELOG.md` under `## Unreleased` to explain that customer-facing incidents now keep their priority uplift outside business hours.
4. Update `docs/priority-rules.md` if needed to keep the written priority rules aligned with the implementation.

## Tests

- A customer-facing major incident opened at night is `P1`, proving night incidents are not downgraded.
- A customer-facing major incident opened on a weekend is `P1`, proving the rule is independent of business-day status.
- Existing severity, maintenance, P1 boundary, and timezone tests continue to pass, proving unrelated priority behavior remains unchanged.
- Existing routing tests continue to pass, proving business hours still affect responder selection rather than priority.

## Risks

- Removing the business-hours condition could accidentally alter maintenance handling or severity boundaries; the existing priority tests cover those paths.
- Changing priority logic could be confused with routing behavior; routing is intentionally left unchanged and its tests provide regression coverage.

## Out of scope

- Responder routing and on-call schedules.
- Maintenance-window definitions.
- Incident APIs, persistence, notifications, and reporting.
